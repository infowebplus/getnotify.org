# GetNotify – Product Story

## Vision

GetNotify is a WhatsApp infrastructure platform built for growing businesses and SaaS teams.

Our mission is simple:

To transform WhatsApp from a basic messaging channel into a structured, event-driven communication layer.

Instead of scattered scripts and fragile integrations, businesses get a centralized system to trigger, monitor and scale operational notifications with full visibility and cost control.

GetNotify is Part of UPHOLD Group.

---

## The Problem

Growing businesses face the same communication challenges:

- Notifications hardcoded across multiple systems
- No unified control over WhatsApp messaging
- Poor visibility into delivery failures
- No cost monitoring per brand or account
- Manual retry processes
- Difficult onboarding when scaling to multiple brands or clients

Agencies face an even bigger problem:

Managing multiple WhatsApp Business Accounts (WABA), multiple brands, and different integrations without a structured infrastructure layer.

The result:
Unpredictable costs. Broken flows. Operational risk.

---

## The Solution

GetNotify provides structured WhatsApp infrastructure built around event-based triggers.

Instead of sending messages manually or embedding fragile scripts, businesses connect their systems once and let events drive communication.

Core capabilities:

- Event-based notification engine
- Standard integrations (Shopify, WooCommerce, booking systems)
- Custom webhook integrations
- Multi-WABA and multi-brand support
- Delivery monitoring dashboard
- Cost tracking per WhatsApp account
- Retry automation logic
- API-first architecture
- Role-based team access

GetNotify is not just a messaging tool.
It is an operational communication layer.

---

## How It Works

1. Connect your WhatsApp Business Account (WABA).
2. Integrate your platform (Shopify, Woo, ERP, CRM or custom system).
3. Define event triggers (e.g. order_confirmed, appointment_created).
4. Monitor delivery, retry failures automatically and track costs.

All managed from a centralized dashboard.
