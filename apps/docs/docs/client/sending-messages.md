# Sending messages

Send WhatsApp notifications to your contacts from the dashboard or via the API.

## From the dashboard

1. Open **Messages** (or **Broadcasts**) in the sidebar.
2. Click **Create** or **New message**.
3. Choose a template or compose a custom message (depending on product support).
4. Select recipients (all contacts, a segment, or a list).
5. Optionally schedule for later.
6. Send.

## Templates

WhatsApp requires approved templates for most business messages. Create templates in the dashboard (or via your WhatsApp Business account) and use them when sending. Custom, non-template messages may be limited by WhatsApp policy.

## Delivery and status

- Check delivery status in the **Messages** view.
- Failed messages may show a reason (e.g. invalid number, template rejected). Update the contact or template and retry.

## Via API

Use the [API](/api/intro) to send messages programmatically. See [Endpoints](/api/endpoints) for the reference.
